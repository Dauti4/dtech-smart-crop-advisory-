📘 Practical Farming Guide

General reminders for field preparation, crop establishment, crop care and harvest.

🚜

1. Prepare the Field

  • Assess drainage and soil condition.
  • Test soil where possible.
  • Arrange suitable seed and inputs.
  • Protect soil structure and organic matter.
🌱

2. Establish the Crop

  • Plant when conditions are suitable.
  • Follow variety-specific spacing and depth.
  • Check germination and missing plants.
  • Control weeds early.
💧

3. Manage Water

  • Monitor soil moisture.
  • Protect fields from waterlogging.
  • Use water efficiently if irrigating.
  • Watch for stress during dry spells.
🔍

4. Monitor Crop Health

  • Inspect for pests and diseases.
  • Check for nutrient deficiency symptoms.
  • Use locally recommended treatments.
  • Seek expert help when unsure.
🌾

5. Harvest and Store

  • Check actual crop maturity.
  • Dry produce adequately.
  • Keep storage areas clean and dry.
  • Protect grain from pests and moisture.
Always follow the seed supplier's instructions and advice from your local agricultural extension officer for crop varieties, fertiliser, pest control and district-specific planting dates.
